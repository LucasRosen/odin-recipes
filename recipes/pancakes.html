Swedish Pancakes

Description

A very simple and fast recipes for classic swedish pancakes.

This recipe will yield around 8-10 pancakes

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 dl flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 6 dl milk
  • 3 eggs
  • butter(for the pan)

Steps

  1. Mix the flour and salt.
  2. Mix in half of the milk and whisk until you have a smooth batter.
  3. Add the rest of the milk and the eggs. Let the batter rest for around 10 minutes.
  4. Fry thin pancakes with a little bit of butter for every pancake.
